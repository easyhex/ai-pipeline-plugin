**Row rules:** a concept is broader than one feature ("realtime collaboration", "GPU offload") — feature-local exclusions stay in that feature's spec. Reasons state the trade-off, not just the verdict ("complexity outweighs the 2% accuracy gain at our data scale"), so a future revisit knows what would have to change. Rows are never deleted; a revisited-and-accepted concept gets a strikethrough plus a link to the accepting spec.
